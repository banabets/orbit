Act · Mail

Pay to reach any wallet, get it back if they ignore you

Messages are encrypted in your browser and stored on Robinhood Chain. If a wallet charges, your payment waits in the contract until they reply or 72 hours pass.

Their rule when they reply
What happens
Back to you
To them
Orbit fee (5% of what they keep)

STEP 1Turn on your inbox

One free signature makes your key. Then set your price and rule.

STEP 2Write to a wallet

Open Mail, paste an address or tap “Message creator” on a token.

STEP 3Reply to collect

Replying settles the payment by your rule. You become contacts.

Try it · Wallet labels
Can Orbit read my messages?

No. They are encrypted in your browser with a key that comes from your wallet signature. The contract and Orbit only see ciphertext.

What if the contract has a bug?

It is not audited yet. Send small amounts while it is new.

Does the ping with Orbit closed say who wrote?

No. It only says you have mail. Open Orbit to read it.
